HER DRAWINGS FOR DRIFT & DISCOVER
=================================

Put pictures in this folder with exactly these names (lowercase). Each one
replaces that part of the sea. Anything missing keeps the original look, so
you can add them one at a time. To go back, delete the file.

General rules
- PNG with a transparent background (export without the paper/background layer).
- Crop tight: the edges of the picture are the edges of the thing. Empty
  margins make it look smaller and off-centre.
- Any size works; the sizes below are plenty for an iPhone screen.
- The app adds the lighting itself (darker at night, foggier far away), so
  draw everything in daylight colours.

Creatures
- whale.png        Side view facing RIGHT. Nose touches the right edge, tail
                   tip the left edge. About 1400 x 500.
- jellyfish.png    Bell at the top, tentacles hanging down. The widest part of
                   the bell spans the full width; the top of the bell touches
                   the top edge. About 500 x 700. It gently squashes as it swims.
- seahorse.png     Upright, facing RIGHT. Top of the head at the top edge, tail
                   at the bottom edge. About 400 x 700. It flips itself to face
                   left when it turns.
  Optional colour versions (these win over the plain one for that creature):
  jellyfish-cyan.png, jellyfish-lilac.png, jellyfish-gold.png,
  jellyfish-ribbon.png, seahorse-coral.png, seahorse-gold.png, seahorse-lilac.png

Things
- bottle.png       Lying on its side, neck pointing RIGHT. Base at the left
                   edge, cork at the right edge. About 600 x 220.
- pearl.png        Round, filling the square. About 160 x 160. Also used as the
                   pearl icon next to the pearl count and prices.

Seabed decorations (shop items)
- kelp.png         One tall kelp plant, root at the bottom centre. About
                   300 x 1000. It sways; several copies stand in the sea,
                   every other one mirrored.
- coral.png        The glowing coral, base at the bottom centre. About 800 x 800.
                   It sways a little.
- chest.png        The treasure chest, front view. About 700 x 500. The bottom
                   tenth sinks into the sand.
- ruins.png        The ancient ruins, standing on the bottom edge. About
                   1400 x 500 (wide).

Background
- background.png (or .jpg)   The painted sea behind everything: water, light,
                   distant hills. Portrait, about 1200 x 2600. It is darkened
                   automatically for sunset and midnight.
- background-day.png, background-sunset.png, background-midnight.png
                   Optional: one painting per light (Brisbane time). Each wins
                   over background.png for its time of day, and they crossfade
                   as the light changes.
  The rocks, seagrass and sand at the very bottom stay as they are, because
  the seahorses hold on to the seagrass and her kept bottles rest on the ledge.

After adding or changing a file, commit it. She sees it the next time she
opens the page (a hard refresh if it was already open).
